What it is & why it matters

A clickable, moveable view through the property.

A 360° camera records a sweeping view of each accessible area of the home. Viewers can click, drag, and look around within the video instead of being limited to a single camera angle.

The result provides a more complete visual record of the property for buyers who want to revisit spaces, family members who could not attend, and listings that benefit from an immersive way to explore the home.

  • 360° capture of accessible rooms
  • Clickable and moveable viewing
  • Broad visual record of the property
  • Shareable digital delivery
