A few things to know

Simple rewards.
Clear rules.

Here’s how your introduction becomes a thank-you.

  1. For past clients. For new friends.

    You must be a past InSites inspection client. Your friend must be a new InSites client, and the referral must be submitted before they book. Self-referrals do not qualify. Real-estate licensees and anyone with a financial interest in the closing or settlement of the referred transaction are not eligible for referral rewards.

  2. They save $25. You earn $25.

    Your friend receives $25 off a full home inspection using your unique referral code when scheduling. You receive a $25 Amazon gift card for each qualifying referral. Standalone add-on services do not qualify. The discount cannot be combined with other promotional discounts unless InSites states otherwise.

  3. Completed and paid comes first.

    The referred inspection must be completed and paid in full. After both conditions are met, there is a 24-hour waiting period before reward fulfillment and eligibility verification. Canceled, unpaid, refunded, or disputed inspections do not qualify.

  4. Your code connects the dots.

    Your code belongs to you and can be shared with multiple friends by text or email. Your friend must enter it when booking. That code connects their inspection to your reward; their booking email does not need to match an email entered here. Your own email must be accurate because that’s where your reward goes. Contact Neal if you need to correct your details.

  5. No limit on good introductions.

    There’s no limit to the number of qualifying friends you can refer. One reward is available per referred new client; credit goes to the eligible owner of the referral code used on the qualifying inspection. Rewards are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash.

  6. Six months to claim your thank-you.

    Claim your $25 Amazon reward within six months of the reward’s issue date. Unclaimed reward links expire after that period. Once claimed for an Amazon gift card, the gift card’s own terms apply; this six-month window applies to claiming the reward.

  7. Permission, privacy, and honest service.

    Get your friend’s permission before sharing their name and email. We use the information to verify eligibility, match their inspection, and administer the referral and reward. We do not share their inspection findings with you. No review or positive rating is required. Rewards do not depend on inspection results or whether a property sale closes.
