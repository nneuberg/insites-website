Meet your inspector

Hi, I’m Neal.

Home inspector. Professional photographer. Lifelong student of the way homes are made—and the people who make them matter.

Rooted here

I believe in this place.

I was born and raised in Trumbull County. It used to be a community I couldn’t wait to leave. Today, it’s a community I can’t wait to stay in.

I grew up alongside a dad who kept me close to construction and fixer-upper projects. That early exposure made me curious about how houses work—the systems behind the walls, the decisions that make a home last, and the details that can quietly become expensive.

After nearly a decade as a professional photographer, real estate brought those interests together. Photography taught me to notice what others miss. Inspection work gave that attention to detail a new purpose: helping people see a property clearly and feel cared for through a major life decision.

“My promise is simple: you’ll leave feeling cared for, with work completed with integrity and professionalism.”
